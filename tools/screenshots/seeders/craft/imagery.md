# Northfold's images

Every picture on the site is an illustration rather than a photograph: soft gradients, layered hills and flat shapes, as if cut from coloured paper and lit by a low sun. Landscape, 16:10, with plenty of sky.

## Journal

- **Look:** soft gradient skies running from pale straw to dusty blue, two or three layers of rolling hills, a low sun near the horizon.
- **Colour:** muted greens, sage and moss, with earth tones: ochre, clay, peat brown. One warm accent at most, such as a russet tree or an allium head.
- **Subjects:** the subject of the entry, simplified: a border, a bench, a pond, a row of pots, seen from a little way off.
- **Avoid:** people's faces, text, logos, hard shadows, bright saturated colour, anything photographic.

## Pages

- **Look:** the same flat, layered style, calmer and wider: open views of the Tyne valley, a walled garden from above, the studio among trees.
- **Colour:** quieter than the journal, mostly greens and soft greys with a pale sky; leave space at the top for a heading to sit over.
- **Subjects:** places and settings rather than objects; no close-ups.
- **Avoid:** busy detail, people, text, anything that would fight with the words laid over it.
