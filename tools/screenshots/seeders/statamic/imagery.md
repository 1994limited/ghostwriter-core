# How Northfold's pictures look

The site's images are illustrations rather than photographs: calm, simple landscapes built from soft gradients and layered hills, in a muted palette. They should feel like the north on a still morning.

## Journal

- **Subject:** a single landscape scene that hints at the piece (a border, an orchard, a walled garden, the coast), never a close-up of a person.
- **Composition:** wide, with three to five layered hills or planting bands receding to a low horizon, and plenty of sky.
- **Light:** low sun, early morning or late afternoon, with a soft glow near the horizon.
- **Colour:** muted greens, sage and moss, with earth tones (ochre, rust, peat brown) and a pale warm sky. No saturated blues or bright flowers.
- **Texture:** smooth gradients and flat shapes, no outlines, no text.
- **Shape:** landscape, about 16:10.

## Pages

- **Subject:** broad, quiet views that suit the studio as a whole: hills, a field edge, a garden path at dusk.
- **Composition:** simpler than the journal, with more empty space so a headline can sit over the top third.
- **Light:** low sun or dusk, warm on the horizon and cooler overhead.
- **Colour:** the same muted greens and earth tones, a shade lighter, so text stays readable.
- **Shape:** wide, about 16:9 for heroes; 4:3 for images within a page.
