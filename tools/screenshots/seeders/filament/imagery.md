# How Northfold's pictures look

## Posts

Wide, calm landscapes rather than close-ups: soft gradient skies over layered hills, two or three bands of land fading into the distance. The sun is low, early morning or late afternoon, so the light is warm and the shadows long.

- **Colour:** muted greens, olive and moss, with earth tones of ochre, rust and peat brown. Skies run from pale straw to dusty blue. Nothing saturated.
- **Composition:** a low horizon, roughly a third of the way up, with space above it. Usually landscape, about 16:10.
- **Mood:** quiet and seasonal. Frost, mist and late summer haze all suit; bright midday sun does not.
- **Avoid:** people posing, tools laid out for the camera, text, and anything that looks like a stock "dream garden".

## Pages

The same soft, layered hills, a little simpler, so a heading can sit over the picture. One clear band of land, a gradient sky, and a low sun just off the frame.

- **Colour:** the same muted greens and earth tones, kept lighter at the top for legibility.
- **Composition:** wide, with the busiest part of the picture in the lower third.
- **Mood:** settled and welcoming, the view from a garden bench rather than a mountain top.
